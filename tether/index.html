THREE PLACES, ONE PRIVATE SPACE

Open it. Connect. Get on with it.

Each device has its own identity. Connect using the other device's reachable address and public key; the other person approves the request. After that, the desktop app keeps connections available while it runs.

  1. SlackPrivate, live one-to-one messages.
  2. MailAn inbox and an outgoing queue that keeps messages for retry.
  3. CloudSend files directly or use encrypted storage on configured cores.

AN OPEN NOTEBOOK

What goes into
the mix?

Your profile starts empty. MusicBrainz resolves the artists you search for, and ListenBrainz supplies recordings connected through listening patterns. The page ranks those candidates by shared paths, community tags, your selected context, discovery level, and your loves and skips. It then spreads results across different artists and tags. The Graph tab displays these same paths as an interactive network of artists, recordings, and tags.

Tags and listening patterns are useful clues, not objective measurements of a song or a person. The app does not analyze audio, lyrics, voices, or life phases directly. It shows where each recommendation came from.

Your local data

When you enable history, we store your artist choices, context choices, likes, skips, and link opens in this browser’s IndexedDB. The page makes no analytics request and sends no activity log to a Music Mosaic server. Artist names you search for go to MusicBrainz; selected artist IDs go to ListenBrainz to fetch candidates. If you open a Spotify or Apple Music link, that provider receives the search request. Hosting services may process ordinary web request logs.

You can pause logging, export or import your profile as JSON, or delete the browser copy in “Manage my data.” An export is an ordinary file and can be read by anyone who gets a copy. Browser storage can be cleared by the browser or device, so export a backup if the history matters to you.

What the site can play

Music Mosaic does not host audio. The current prototype opens a search for each pick in Spotify or Apple Music. In-page account playback requires separate developer registrations and provider authorization; it is not active in this build. Music discovery works without either account.

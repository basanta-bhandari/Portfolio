Your browser identity

Names aren’t verified accounts. Your keys and joined rooms stay in this tab’s session; closing it may end access to DMs. Keep a room’s invite to rejoin.

Privacy and connections

Direct connections share your network address with the other participant. Turn this off to use encrypted delivery through the site.

Messages are encrypted before leaving your browser. Direct connections use WebRTC; encrypted delivery through this site keeps chat working when a direct connection isn’t available. The service sees room names, participant names, timing, and connection metadata. It doesn’t receive the room encryption key or plaintext messages.

Session keys aren’t protected by a separate passphrase. Don’t use a shared browser profile for sensitive conversations. Browser chat is separate from the native Tether mesh for now.

Room details

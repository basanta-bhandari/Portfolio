Instructions!

For requesters

  1. Post your request in #printing-legion! Include your country, the STL files to print, and a picture of what you need
  2. Go to printlegion.hackclub.com and reach out to someone! Ask them to print your stuff
  3. Pay for the shipping label using your YSWS card grant. You do not get extra money for this!
  4. Repeat!

For printers:

  1. Sign up by filling out forms.hackclub.com/printing-legion
  2. Wait for someone to reach out! If you want a job sooner, feel free to grab any you see in #printing-legion
  3. Print their part! Once you do, ping them in #printing-legion with a picture of their print!
  4. Ship it - Ask for the requester’s info and use their HCB card grant to pay for the label
  5. Fill out https://forms.hackclub.com/sent-print
  6. Once you’ve printed 750g of filament & shipped it all out, you'll get a $20 HCB card grant to buy more filament!
  7. Repeat!

FAQ:

Can I submit personal prints?

Nope! Printing Legion is only for Hack Club YSWS programs!

How does filament reimbursement work?

For every 750g of filament you print and ship, you get a $20 HCB card grant to get 1 new spool of filament!

What other rewards do I get?

Glad you asked! Apart from filament reimbursement, you can also unlock rewards based on how many print requests you send out:

  • 3 print jobs: a $25 Packing material grant! (this one refreshed every 9 print jobs)
  • 6 print jobs: a $30 sticker grant to get stickers from Jukebox!
  • 12 print jobs: a $60 grant to get a label printer! This makes life 10x easier

Note that these are counted per project, not by number of prints (i.e a hackpad with 3 parts still counts as 1 print!)
